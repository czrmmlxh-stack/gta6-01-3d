Sunvale 3D, a 3D open-world crime game: drive, steal cars, dodge the police and run Marla's missions. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6” bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

Sunvale is a sun-soaked coastal city where Marla runs the crew and you are her new driver. Twenty jobs take you from borrowing your first car to ending the Viper War: steal rides, run deliveries, shake three- and four-star dragnets, and finally burn the Vipers' nest at the abandoned casino. On foot or behind the wheel, with traffic, pedestrians, police chases, a police helicopter, roadblocks, a repair garage, and a respect system that tracks your rise — all in three.js, all offline, no external assets.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8901/game/index.html — WASD to move and drive, E to enter and exit cars, F to punch, G for the pistol, Space to brake, Shift to run, P to pause.
